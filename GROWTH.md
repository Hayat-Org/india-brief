# Growing a brand-new account: what's built in and what you do

No tool can promise reach, especially for brand-new accounts. What we can do is make every post as good as it can be, post when India is reading, and avoid anything that gets new accounts demoted.

## Built into the system
- **Ranking that rewards what matters to readers.** Score = 35% criticality + 30% importance + 35% popularity, plus small bonuses for: trending in India right now (free Google Trends signal), high-criticality, high-importance, and multiple outlets covering it. All topic-neutral. Popularity is about broad usefulness, never outrage.
- **Hook-first writing.** Each post opens with the single most important fact in plain words, then what happened, who's affected and what's next. No clickbait, no engagement bait.
- **India peak slots:** 07:35, 12:35, 18:35, 21:05 IST, plus fast-track for confirmed breaking stories.
- **Daily "Today in short" carousel** at ~22:10 IST: a swipeable Instagram carousel (carousels earn saves and swipes), plus Facebook and Threads versions with links.
- **Accessibility and cleanliness:** alt text on every image, ≤5 hashtags, no all-caps, consistent branded look.
- **Balance cap:** at most 2 Politics posts/day so the feed stays broad (weather, sports, health, economy, science, states).

## One trade-off to test
Meta's guidance (as summarised by security press) lists external links among low-quality signals, but our Facebook and Threads posts link to the original report for credit and attribution, which is also good legal hygiene. We keep links **on** by default. After a few weeks, you can set the secret `ATTACH_LINKS=0` for a week and compare reach. The outlet is still named in every post.

## What you do (10-15 minutes a day)
1. **Reply to comments** politely and neutrally, especially in the first hour after a post. Never argue. Hide abuse.
2. **Complete every profile:** photo, bio, contact e-mail, link between the three accounts (Instagram bio links Threads and the Facebook Page).
3. **Follow relevant accounts** manually (news outlets, regional journalists) and join conversations genuinely.
4. **Share to Stories/Notes by hand** when something big runs. Stories can't be automated safely yet.
5. **Weeks 1-4:** keep review mode on, approve quickly (reach decays), and note which posts get saves and shares.

## Don't (these hurt new accounts)
Buying followers; follow/unfollow loops; engagement pods; asking people to like/share/tag; link-only posts; more than 5 hashtags; reposting others' photos; changing the name/handle repeatedly; posting 20 times a day.
